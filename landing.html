How the terminal actually works

Three steps, every trading day. None of them involve willpower alone.

1

Lock the rules before the open

Set your max risk, your setups, your no-trade windows — before price moves your judgement. Once the session starts, the rules are the rules.

2

Log the trade as you take it

Every entry, exit and reason gets written down in real time, in the same journal — not reconstructed from memory that night, flattering yourself along the way.

3

Get scored, not soothed

SciFe reviews the session against the rules you set and tells you plainly whether you kept your word. No spreadsheet you can quietly edit after the fact.

What's inside the terminal

One workspace for the whole trading day — not six tabs and a spreadsheet.

Journal & calendar

Every trade, tagged by setup and session, laid out on a calendar so patterns in your own behaviour are impossible to miss.

Risk rules engine

The confluence panel above — drag your weekly risk budget and watch the verdict change from "room to work" to "pushing it," live.

SciFe, your AI coach

Trained on your own journal, SciFe answers questions about your patterns and calls out rule breaks — it doesn't just cheerlead.

Analytics & playbook

Win rate, expectancy and drawdown broken down by setup, so you know which parts of your plan are actually working.

Backtest & strategy notes

Test a rule change against your own trade history before you risk live money finding out it doesn't hold up.

Prop firm compliance

A dedicated room that tracks your account against your prop firm's drawdown and consistency rules, so a breach doesn't blindside you.

Before you message us

The questions almost everyone asks before their first session.

Why invitation only?

Ghost Discipline started as a private tool inside GMC Traders. It stayed invite-only on purpose — every member is vouched for by someone already inside, which keeps the rooms full of people who actually take their rules seriously.

What is SciFe?

SciFe is the AI coach built into every plan. It reads your journal, not a generic script, and answers questions about your own trading patterns — including the ones you'd rather it not notice.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. The Free plan is a genuine starting point, not a trial — the journal, calendar and rate-limited SciFe access don't expire. Message the Telegram whenever you want Pro or Elite unlocked.

How does billing actually work?

There's no card form. You message the Telegram with the plan you want, and the team upgrades your account by hand and arranges payment directly with you. You can downgrade or cancel the same way, any time.

Is this financial advice?

No. Ghost Discipline is a risk-management and journaling tool — it helps you follow rules you set for yourself. It doesn't tell you what to trade, and nothing in the terminal should be treated as investment advice.
