A better trading routine

Work the process.
Let the result speak.

Structure gives the session a beginning, a record, and a useful close. Ghost Discipline keeps those pieces together in your workspace.

01 / PREPARE

Set the boundaries

Write down balance, risk limits, setups, session windows, and cooling-off rules before the market gets loud.

02 / EXECUTE

Record the decisions

Log entries, exits, context, and state of mind close to the moment. Keep the facts beside the plan they came from.

03 / REVIEW

Learn from the record

Review performance and discipline together. Find which habits support your edge and which ones quietly tax it.

A serious journal
should tell the truth.

A trading journal is only useful when it records the decisions that shaped the outcome. Ghost Discipline brings risk settings, orders, notes, psychology, and review into the same workflow.

It does not give signals or promise returns. It helps you see your own behavior more clearly—and make the next decision with the full record in view.

A few useful details

Before you get started.

How do I create an account?

Create a free account from the terminal sign-in screen. Your journal is saved to your account.

Does Ghost Discipline tell me what to trade?

No. It is a journaling, risk-management, and review tool. It does not provide trading signals or investment advice.

Can I start on the Free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes the core journal, calendar, orders, rules, psychology tools, and a limited amount of SciFe chat.

How do paid upgrades work?

Choose a package in your account and complete payment through Paystack. A successful payment grants 30 days of access; renewal is manual.

Can teammates see every trade?

Team desk review shares a selected weekly summary. It is not a standing view into every trade in your journal.
