What This Journal Is For

Ghost Discipline exists to make you accountable to the rules you set for yourself before emotion gets a vote. Every trade you log, every rule you break or hold, and every check-in you complete feeds a single outcome: honest proof of whether you traded the plan, or just traded the moment.

Over weeks and months this becomes a record you can't argue with — your discipline score, your rule violations, your real win rate under your own rules. It isn't here to flatter you. It's here to keep score honestly, so the next version of you trades better than the last.

Request a Review

Reviews normally pop up on their own at the start of a new week or month. Don't want to wait? Pull up the last completed week or month whenever you want an honest look at how you traded.
