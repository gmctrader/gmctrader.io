How the terminal actually works

Three steps, every trading day. None of them involve willpower alone.

1

Lock the rules before the open

Set your max risk, your setups, your no-trade windows — before price moves your judgement. Once the session starts, the rules are the rules.

2

Log the trade as you take it

Every entry, exit and reason gets written down in real time, in the same journal — not reconstructed from memory that night, flattering yourself along the way.

3

Get scored, not soothed

SciFe reviews the session against the rules you set and tells you plainly whether you kept your word. No spreadsheet you can quietly edit after the fact.

The rule was never the hard part

Anyone can write down "risk 1% per trade" at 6am, coffee in hand, market closed. The rule breaks at 11:47am, three losses deep, when the setup that just cost you money shows up again and every instinct says this time it's different. Ghost Discipline doesn't stop you from taking that trade. It just makes sure you can't quietly forget you took it — or convince yourself later that the rule didn't really apply that day.

That's the whole design. Not a smarter strategy, not a signal to follow — a mirror that doesn't flatter you. The seal is either sealed or it's cracked. SciFe reads your actual journal, not a story you tell it. And every trader who joins was vouched for by someone who's already sat with that mirror and kept coming back anyway.

Before you message us

The questions almost everyone asks before their first session.

Why invitation only?

Ghost Discipline started as a private tool inside GMC Traders. It stayed invite-only on purpose — every member is vouched for by someone already inside, which keeps the rooms full of people who actually take their rules seriously.

What is SciFe?

SciFe is the AI coach built into every plan. It reads your journal, not a generic script, and answers questions about your own trading patterns — including the ones you'd rather it not notice.

Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. The Free plan is a genuine starting point, not a trial — the journal, calendar and rate-limited SciFe access don't expire. Message the Telegram whenever you want Pro, Elite, or Premium unlocked.

How does billing actually work?

There's no card form. You message the Telegram with the plan you want, and the team upgrades your account by hand and arranges payment directly with you. You can downgrade or cancel the same way, any time.

What happens when my plan runs out?

You'll see a reminder in the terminal starting 3 days before your renewal date. If it lapses without being renewed, the account simply drops back to Free — nothing is deleted, your journal and history stay exactly as they are, and you keep the core rooms. Message the Telegram whenever you're ready to pick the paid tier back up.

Is this financial advice?

No. Ghost Discipline is a risk-management and journaling tool — it helps you follow rules you set for yourself. It doesn't tell you what to trade, and nothing in the terminal should be treated as investment advice.

What actually happens when I break a rule?

Nothing punitive — no lockout, no lecture popup. The seal on your Rules room flips from sealed to cracked, and that break sits in your journal exactly as it happened, tagged and dated. SciFe will bring it up when it's relevant, and your Weekly Desk Review (if you're on a team) shows it to your teammates too. The discomfort of a visible, permanent record is the entire mechanism — nobody's watching over your shoulder except the version of you that has to read it back later.

Can teammates see my full journal, or just the weekly summary?

Just the weekly summary. Your desk partners see the Weekly Desk Review — win rate, R, rule breaks, routine scores — for the week you've selected. They don't get a standing window into every trade you've ever logged. You control what's shared by choosing what week to open for review, and nothing syncs until you accept a team invite in the first place.
